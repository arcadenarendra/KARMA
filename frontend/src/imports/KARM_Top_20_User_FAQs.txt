KARM - TOP 20 USER FAQs

1. What is KARM?
KARM is a civic platform where citizens can report local problems, share evidence, and publicly track their resolution.

2. What kind of problems can I report?
You can report issues related to Education, Healthcare, Municipal Works, and Public Safety.

3. How do I report a problem?
Create a report, describe the issue, and provide photo or video evidence to support it.

4. Can I report a problem anonymously?
Yes. You can choose to hide your identity when submitting a report.

5. Will my location be visible if I upload a photo?
KARM removes GPS and device-related metadata from uploaded photos before they are published.

6. Why should I provide photo or video evidence?
Evidence helps demonstrate that the reported issue is genuine and allows other citizens to better understand the problem.

7. Can other people support my report?
Yes. Other users can upvote your report and share their own experiences with the same issue.

8. What does “Days Unresolved” mean?
It shows how long a reported problem has remained unresolved since it was submitted.

9. Who will see my report?
Reports are publicly visible so citizens, journalists, and relevant authorities can see and track them.

10. How does my report reach the concerned authority?
Reports are organised by category so the relevant government department can view issues related to its area and respond to them.

11. Can I track what happens after I report an issue?
Yes. You can follow the report's status and see how long it remains unresolved.

12. What happens when the problem is fixed?
The concerned authority can mark the issue as resolved, and the report remains in the platform's history with its resolution timeline.

13. What if the authority does not respond?
The Days Unresolved counter continues to run publicly, making the lack of response visible.

14. What if someone has already reported the same problem?
You can support the existing report by upvoting it and adding your experience instead of creating another duplicate report.

15. Can I report fake or misleading information?
Yes. Community flagging and moderation are used to identify potentially false or inappropriate reports.

16. How does KARM prevent spam and abusive content?
The platform uses rule-based filtering and community flagging to identify spam, hate speech, personal information, and other inappropriate content.

17. Is my personal information safe?
KARM aims to minimise stored personal information, particularly for anonymous reports, and uses authentication and privacy measures to protect users.

18. Is KARM replacing government complaint systems?
No. KARM is designed to make civic problems and government responses more visible and accountable rather than replacing the authorities' role.

19. How is KARM different from social media?
Unlike ordinary social media posts that can quickly disappear from attention, KARM keeps civic reports organised, visible, and trackable until resolution.

20. Why should I use KARM instead of simply complaining online?
KARM gives your complaint a public record, supporting evidence, community upvotes, and an unresolved timer, making it easier for an issue to remain visible until action is taken.
